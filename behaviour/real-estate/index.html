The problem
Portal fees went up. Your hit rate didn't.

You pay for every enquiry. Your team gets more of them than it has time to call, and picks by gut. The vendor who would have listed this week rings another agent on Thursday. You'll never know which one it was.

Your CRM stores the data. It doesn't tell anyone what to do with it.

I

Calling the wrong enquiries.

Volume is high, signal is low. Negotiators prioritise by gut, not by what the enquiry has done. The warm ones go cold while the cold ones get the call.

II

Missing the right moment.

The window between "interested" and "ready" is short and quiet. By the time someone asks again, another agency has already replied.

III

Not knowing what to say.

Even with the right enquiry at the right time, the wrong opener kills the conversation. The opener has to come from what the person did.

How it works

Not a black box. Plain logic you can read.

Aletheai RE sits on top of the CRM you already use and turns what it records into a short, explained list. Three steps, each one visible.

SIGNALS

What the enquiry did.

Returned to a listing. Booked a viewing and never confirmed it. A mortgage decision in principle arrived. Search criteria moved. Days of silence. Your CRM already records these; it just doesn't act on them.

PATTERNS

What that usually means.

Signals are read as patterns a negotiator would recognise. Each one is a reason you can say out loud, not a probability.

DECISIONS

Who, why, what next.

Patterns become decisions through logic you can read. The output is a ranked list with the reason written on every entry, so when a manager asks "why this one?" there is a straight answer.
