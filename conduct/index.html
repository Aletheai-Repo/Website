The problem
You gave an agent a job. You also gave it your keys.

To be useful, an agent needs access to your systems, your accounts, your customers. Once it has access, it can do things nobody approved. Not out of malice: because it inferred, or guessed.

You are accountable for what your agents do. Today, you have their word for it. An explanation is not evidence. An agent can describe what it did, and why, in fluent prose — and the description can be wrong, incomplete, or written after the fact. A board, a regulator or an insurer cannot act on it, and neither should you.

As AI reasoning becomes less human-readable, reading it stops being a form of oversight at all. What survives is oversight of what the agent actually did: which acts it attempted, which were allowed, which were refused, and on what evidence. We audit conduct, not thought.

Your agents are not the risk. Unbounded authority is.

What it does

Every act, through a gate the agent cannot reach.

Aletheai Conduct sits between your agents and the systems they act on. The effect is three things.

I

The gate is out of the agent's reach.

Every act an agent takes passes through it, and the agent cannot reach it.

II

The act is registered before it happens.

The act goes on the record first, so the record can never be a story written afterwards.

III

The act must be justified by evidence the agent did not write.

What an agent says about its own request is not evidence. The justification has to come from evidence the agent did not write.

Not a guardrail
Guardrails watch what an agent says. Aletheai Conduct governs what it does.

Filters check words on the way in and out. A word is not an act.

Aletheai Conduct sits where the act happens, not where the words are. A request can read perfectly and still be refused.

What we believe
We would rather tell you we cannot assess something than guess.

A record you can defend has to include the gaps. We built Aletheai Conduct on the view that a confident answer with nothing behind it is worse than no answer, and that the people who carry the accountability deserve to know which is which.

What you get

The Assurance Report.

  • What your agents did — every act that passed the gate, in the period.
  • What was refused, and why — in plain language, not in the agent's words.
  • What evidence each act rested on.
  • What a person approved, and when.
  • What could not be assessed, with the reason — stated, not hidden.

Re-derivable from the underlying record, identically, by you or by anyone you hand it to. The report is the product; everything else exists to make it true.

Questions you will have

Is this monitoring? Does it help with the EU AI Act?

Is this a monitoring tool?

No. Monitoring tells you what went wrong. Aletheai Conduct holds the act before it lands, and records why.

Does it help with the EU AI Act?

It gives you human oversight and a decision record by design. It is not legal advice, and it is not certification.
